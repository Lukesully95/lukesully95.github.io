The property

A mansion
in the sky.

Discreet from the street and formidable in scale, the residence occupies floors 22 through 25 of one of Fifth Avenue's most private co-ops.

A key-locked private elevator opens to a walnut and green-marble foyer. The principal rooms face west over Central Park, with Midtown views to the south. Walnut, marble and bronze run through every floor. The residence has seven bedrooms, eight full and two half baths, a library, a screening room, a gym, staff quarters and a private roof terrace with a copper-and-glass conservatory.

The building offers a 24-hour doorman, a resident manager, private storage and a wine cellar. Board approval is required. Brokers, please call for an appointment.
